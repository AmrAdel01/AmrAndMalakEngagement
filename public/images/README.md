# Images go here

Replace these placeholder-referenced files with your real photos (keep the same filenames, or update the paths in `src/data/eventData.js`):

- `childhood-him.jpg` — his childhood photo
- `childhood-her.jpg` — her childhood photo
- `photo-1.jpg` through `photo-6.jpg` — gallery photos (add/remove as many as you like; just update the `images.gallery` array in eventData.js)
- `og-cover.jpg` — the image shown when your link is shared on WhatsApp/social (recommended size: 1200×630px)

Until you add real files, the site shows an elegant placeholder card instead of a broken image — so it's safe to deploy before photos are ready.
